Professional Summary

I began my career in software development at Play Intelligent Systems, working on Industry 4.0 solutions for the packaging industry. I contributed to an end-to-end platform that collected data from industrial machines and used optimization algorithms and AI to improve production planning and dispatch operations.

I am a Data Scientist at Americanas, one of Brazil’s leading retail and e-commerce companies. I work primarily with Pricing Analytics, developing data-driven solutions that support pricing decisions across physical stores. My work also involves assortment planning and retail modulation, connecting data science, business strategy, and large-scale operations.

My work sits at the intersection of data science, engineering, and business. I enjoy turning complex problems into practical solutions, collaborating across disciplines, and building systems that deliver measurable value. Alongside my professional work, I am deepening my focus on AI Engineering, especially LLM applications and agentic systems.

Outside technology, I enjoy playing piano, riding motorcycles, strength training, and cooking.
